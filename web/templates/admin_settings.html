Sales tax

Rates apply to the item subtotal at payment (the tip is not taxed). Set a rate to 0 to switch that tax off. The numbers print on the receipt.

Auto-gratuity

Automatically suggest a gratuity for large parties at payment. Set the party size to 0 to switch it off. Staff can still change it per bill.

Service charge

A mandatory house charge added to every bill (a percent of the items, after any discount). This is the restaurant's revenue, separate from the waiter's tip, and is shown as its own line on the receipt. Set to 0 to switch it off.

Card transaction fee

An optional surcharge added only when the guest pays by card, to pass on the processing cost. A percent of the pre-tip bill. It's shown to the guest on the payment screen and as its own line on the receipt. Cash payments are never surcharged. Set to 0 to switch it off.

Receipt identity

The heading printed on every receipt.
